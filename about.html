About Me

Biography

Patrick Ruckman is a versatile guitarist, multi-instrumentalist, and instructor based in Oak Lawn, Illinois, in the southwest suburbs of Chicago. His stylistic range spans jazz, fusion, rock, R&B, pop, and more — demonstrating a dynamic musical language shaped by influences including Steely Dan, The Beatles, Grant Green, Stevie Wonder, David Gilmour, Pat Metheny, Snarky Puppy, and Freddie Green, just to name a few.

Patrick's education in music spans from the historic lineage of the music educators in the Southwest Suburban Catholic schools, to studying musical performance at UIC. Complimenting his artistic training, he also holds a degree in Psychology, and is a certified Software Developer with multiple credentials — a unique background that informs his approach to student mentorship and his proficiency with studio technology.

With more than two decades of hands-on experience in performance, Patrick’s background as a software developer give him unique insight into music production, music software, and other electrical aspects of being a musician. His advanced level of instrumental skill is matched by a deep understanding of music theory and a genuine drive to create, perform, and share his works.

Currently serving as the jazz guitarist for the Moraine Valley Community Jazz Ensemble, Patrick brings the rhythmic and improvisational spirit of jazz to every performance. He’s also a dedicated member of the Moraine Valley Community Concert Band, being one of many Tuba players — a testament to his years of musical curiosity and commitment.

Patrick is now stepping into a new chapter: performing, teaching guitar to the next generation, and providing guidance for musicians interested in leveraging technology for their craft. His mission is to foster a resurgence in the musical arts, helping build vibrant music communities and supporting new talent through education and collaboration.

Whether on stage, in the studio, or in the classroom, Patrick Ruckman delivers artistry built on devotion, professionalism, and a lifetime of learning and exploration.
